Process

How a shoot comes together

01

Tell Us Your Requirement

Message us your event date, location and the kind of shoot you're after — wedding, pre-wedding, coming-of-age or event coverage.

02

Discuss & Plan

We talk through coverage, styling, timing and locations, and confirm a plan and quote that fits your event.

03

Create

On the day, we shoot candidly and attentively — capturing the moments you'll want to relive, without getting in the way of your celebration.

04

Deliver
